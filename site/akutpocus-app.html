AkutPOCUS App

Ultrasound education and reference for healthcare professionals and medical students.

Explore point-of-care ultrasound (POCUS) with images, videos and practical reference content for emergency medicine, ultrasound examinations and ultrasound-guided procedures.

Download on the App Store ↗Get it on Google Play ↗

Start with the free content

eFAST, Lungs and thorax, and Arterial line are available without a subscription. Explore the scanning guidance, images and videos before choosing full access.

Monthly or annual full access

A monthly or annual subscription unlocks the remaining reference content. Both plans include the same content. Purchases, renewals and cancellation are managed through the App Store or Google Play; the current price is shown in your store before purchase.

Learn and revisit ultrasound techniques

Browse examination and procedural reference material, with ultrasound images and videos to support learning. AkutPOCUS is an educational reference; it does not analyse patient images or measurements or produce patient-specific diagnoses.
