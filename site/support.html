About SonoNordic

SonoNordic is a Swedish company developing digital solutions for ultrasound education and clinical practice.

Our aim is to make ultrasound knowledge more accessible and support learning and knowledge sharing across healthcare. Our current projects include AkutPOCUS App and AkutPOCUS Clinical.
