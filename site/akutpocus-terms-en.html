Provider and content

The provider is SonoNordic AB, company number 559586-5816, Åbroddsvägen 31D, 132 37 Saltsjö-Boo, Sweden. Contact: info@sononordic.se. AkutPOCUS is an educational and reference app for healthcare professionals and medical students. It presents general content, does not record patient information and does not provide patient-specific diagnoses or treatment recommendations. It does not replace training, local procedures or independent clinical judgement.

Free and full access

All of eFAST, the lung and thorax organ section with lung findings, and arterial line content are free. The BLUE protocol and other paid sections require full access. Monthly and annual subscriptions provide the same content. App information, privacy, terms, support and restoring purchases do not require a subscription.

Price and payment

The Swedish base price is SEK 49 per month or SEK 399 per year. The annual price is paid in one amount. The price, currency and taxes shown in the store's purchase confirmation apply to your purchase; other markets may have different prices. Payment is through Apple App Store or Google Play and is linked to the store account. There is no trial unless expressly offered at purchase.

Automatic renewal and cancellation

Your subscription renews automatically for another month or year unless you turn off renewal in your Apple or Google account. Follow the store's deadline; we recommend turning off renewal at least 24 hours before the period ends. Ordinary cancellation leaves access available until the paid period ends. Cancellation alone does not refund remaining time, but statutory rights apply. Uninstalling or deleting data does not cancel the subscription. Price changes are notified and require consent where applicable; they do not retroactively change a period already paid for.

Offline access

After an online check, you can use stored content until the actual verified expiry date, including for a full annual subscription. A network error alone does not remove a valid stored period. Renewal, restoring purchases and correcting invalid local status may require a connection. After expiry without verified renewal, access must be verified again. Free sections remain available. Refunds or revocation may end access when status is updated. Changing the device clock may trigger a new check.

Restoring purchases and ambassador access

Use Restore purchases with the same store account used for purchase. There is no SonoNordic login or promised automatic transfer between Apple and Google. Contact us if you have problems. Ambassador access is granted for one year at a time, may be extended, expires on the agreed date and does not automatically become paid access. Changing phones or reinstalling requires manual assistance for these grants; provide your support ID.
